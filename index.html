Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, no hype.

Do you guarantee YouTube monetization?

No. We help your channel work toward the eligibility criteria, but YouTube makes the final decision after reviewing the channel.

Do you use bots or fake subscribers?

No. Our services are built around genuine audience-focused promotion. We do not recommend bots, fake accounts, or artificial engagement.

Can you promote my existing videos?

Yes. We can promote selected videos based on your goals and target audience.

Can you help with a new YouTube channel?

Yes. We can set up and optimize a new channel and develop a growth strategy.

How long does growth take?

There is no fixed timeline. Results depend on your content, niche, audience, competition, campaign budget, and other factors.

Can you guarantee a specific number of views or subscribers?

No. Performance varies from channel to channel, so specific results cannot be guaranteed.

Will advertising automatically make my channel monetized?

No. Paid advertising can increase exposure and reach, but YouTube decides monetization eligibility according to its current policies and review process.
